FAQ

Have a Question?

How does the NO GAP offer work?

Our NO GAP offer applies to check-ups and cleans for patients with private health insurance. If your health fund coverage exceeds $180, you won't have to pay any out-of-pocket costs. For those without health insurance, the cost for a comprehensive check-up and clean, including x-rays and fluoride treatment, is $270.

Are payment plans available?

Yes. We accept all major credit cards including Amex, provide onsite HICAPS facilities for health fund claims, offer payment plans through Humm, and accept government programs like the Child Dental Benefits Schedule (CDBS) and Oral Health Care Vouchers.

How do I book an appointment?

Book online 24/7 through our easy-to-use booking system, or call us on (03) 9041 6384. We also offer same-day appointments if we have availability on the day.

Do you see children?

Yes, we do dental for kids of all ages, from infants and toddlers to young children, tweens and teens, and we accept the Child Dental Benefits Schedule (CDBS) for eligible children.
